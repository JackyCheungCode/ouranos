01 / REPORT CONTENTWhat the report covers.The report distinguishes source data, engineering observations and items that still require confirmation.
01

Vehicle identity

Market, make, model, year, derivative and trim information, with VIN decoding optional.

02

Wheel geometry

Diameter, width, offset, PCD, centre bore, load information and front / rear stagger.

03

Clearance review

Brake caliper, spoke profile, barrel clearance, suspension and bodywork considerations.

04

Tyre & hardware

Tyre dimensions, rolling diameter, fastener seat, thread and hub interface requirements.

05

Risk & actions

Missing data, conflicting specifications, modification risks and items requiring manual confirmation.

06

Decision record

Verified, conditional or not verified status, with reviewer notes and source references where available.

02 / REPORT ACCESSTwo levels of information.Customers receive a concise fitment brief. The internal engineering report retains detailed source evidence, review notes, risk flags and decision history for authorised OURANOS staff.

Customer brief

Vehicle and wheel summary, verification status, key conditions and recommended next action.

Internal engineering record

Full source payload, assumptions, dimensional checks, risk classification, reviewer identity and audit trail.

03 / REVIEW PRINCIPLESNo compatibility claim without evidence.

VIN is optional

VIN may help identify a vehicle derivative, but it does not replace verification of modifications, brakes or wheel hardware.

Risk triggers review

Conflicting, incomplete or non-standard configurations are flagged for human engineering review before a final recommendation.

Data sources are disclosed

OEM catalogue data, user-supplied information and engineering assumptions are labelled separately. An API response alone is not an installation approval.
