02 / UNDERSTANDING SPECIFICATIONS Read the complete wheel specification.

PCD · Pitch Circle Diameter

The number of mounting holes and the diameter of the circle passing through their centres. Both must match the vehicle hub.

ET · Offset

Determines the mounting face position relative to the wheel centreline. Changes affect inner clearance and outer position.

CB · Centre Bore

The wheel centre opening. A smaller bore cannot pass over the hub; a larger bore requires an approved centring solution where applicable.

Fastener specification

Thread, seat geometry, engagement and tightening torque must be confirmed for the vehicle and wheel combination.

03 / BRAKE CLEARANCE Clearance is a three-dimensional question. A diameter and offset comparison alone cannot confirm brake clearance. The complete wheel profile and brake package must be considered.
01

Caliper envelope

Radial and axial space around the brake caliper, including the installed brake package.

02

Spoke profile

Spoke curvature and rear-face geometry determine clearance to the caliper at critical points.

03

Barrel profile

Inner barrel geometry and drop-centre shape must clear the brake assembly.

04

Engineering tolerance

Manufacturing tolerances and required running clearance must be included in the final assessment.

Brake clearance is not confirmed by catalogue dimensions alone. Missing brake or wheel-envelope data requires engineering review.

06 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS Fitment questions, answered.
What does wheel offset (ET) mean?

Offset is the distance in millimetres between the wheel centreline and its mounting face. A change in offset moves the wheel inward or outward, affecting suspension and body clearance.

What is PCD and why must it match?

PCD describes the number of mounting holes and their pitch-circle diameter. The wheel’s mounting pattern must match the vehicle hub; a different PCD is not an acceptable fitment.

Can a larger centre bore be used?

A larger centre bore may be possible with an approved hub-centric centring solution. The ring, hub and wheel combination must be checked. A bore smaller than the hub will not fit.

Can a larger wheel clear my brakes?

Not necessarily. Diameter alone does not confirm clearance. Caliper shape, spoke profile, barrel geometry and engineering tolerances must be evaluated.

What is a staggered wheel setup?

A staggered setup uses different front and rear wheel dimensions. Tyre compatibility and rolling circumference must be checked against the vehicle and drivetrain requirements.

Can I replace only one wheel?

Single-wheel replacement depends on matching the exact product, dimensions, finish, load rating and engineering revision. Contact OURANOS with your existing wheel details.

Do I need to check TPMS?

Yes. Confirm sensor compatibility, valve requirements and the vehicle’s TPMS relearn procedure before installation.

How do I know if a wheel is compatible?

Start with the exact vehicle modification, then verify mounting, wheel geometry, brake clearance, tyre and load requirements against the specific wheel variant.
