01 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONSStart with the essentials.
Which wheel dimensions do I need to confirm?

Confirm diameter, width, offset, PCD, centre bore and load rating. Also check the vehicle derivative, brake package, fasteners and tyre specification. Do not rely on bolt pattern alone.

Can I change the spoke design of a forged wheel?

Each OURANOS forged model has a defined design architecture. Configuration may include dimensions, offset, centre bore, PCD, finish, colour, engraving and centre-cap details; changing one spoke design into another is not part of the standard configuration.

What is the minimum order quantity for forged wheels?

Forged wheels are bespoke programmes with a minimum order of four wheels.

What is the minimum order quantity for cast wheels?

Confirmed in-stock cast references may be considered for smaller quantities. Out-of-stock production orders have a minimum order quantity of 100 pieces.

Does a vehicle fitment result guarantee installation clearance?

No. Catalogue data is a starting point. Brake caliper clearance, spoke profile, barrel clearance, tyre pairing, suspension, bodywork and modifications must be confirmed for the exact vehicle.

Can I order a single replacement cast wheel?

Single-wheel replacement may be possible for an in-stock reference, subject to matching the exact model, part reference, size, offset, PCD, centre bore and finish. Availability must be confirmed.

How should I care for the wheel finish?

Use a finish-compatible pH-neutral wheel cleaner and soft non-abrasive tools. Avoid harsh acids, aggressive solvents and cleaning hot wheels. Follow the care instructions supplied for the specific coating.

Can OURANOS support OEM / ODM projects?

Yes. Share the target vehicle or application, technical requirements, target market, annual volume, finish expectations and validation plan. Feasibility and programme scope are reviewed individually.

What information should I include in an enquiry?

Include wheel model, construction, quantity, vehicle make / model / year / trim, intended use, wheel dimensions, finish and any modifications or special requirements.
