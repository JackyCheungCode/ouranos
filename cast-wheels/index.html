01 / ENGINEERING RESPONSE Eliminating the Three Risks of Conventional Cast Wheels. Most cast wheel failures stem from hidden internal porosity, excessive unsprung mass, and premature coating delamination. Here is how a controlled manufacturing line is designed to address each risk at its source.
01 / INTERNAL STRUCTURE
NON-DESTRUCTIVE INSPECTION
STRUCTURAL DENSITY Zero Hidden Porosity. Closed-Loop Low-Pressure Casting. Conventional gravity casting relies on weight alone to fill the mold, trapping air pockets that become fracture points under pothole impact. Closed-loop low-pressure machines inject molten A356.2 aluminum upward under regulated pressure—designed to produce a dense, uniform grain structure. Each production barrel should undergo the specified X-ray internal inspection and helium leak testing before CNC machining. A356.2 Virgin Aluminum · T6 Heat Treated · 100% X-Ray Verification Scope*
02 / LOAD DISTRIBUTION
FEA-LED MATERIAL OPTIMISATION
UNSPRUNG MASS Strength Where It Matters. Weight Removed Where It Doesn't. Overbuilding spoke thickness is a shortcut used to mask weak casting quality—at the cost of sluggish steering and braking. Using Finite Element Analysis (FEA), engineers simulate cornering, radial, and 13°/90° impact loads to identify low-stress zones for mass reduction while reinforcing the spoke-to-rim transition. 03 / SUPPLY & AFTER-SALES Built to Eliminate Your Supply & Fitment Headaches. A great wheel means nothing if it rubs a brake caliper, arrives late, or leaves your customer stranded when one wheel hits a curb. Every order should be supported by traceable serial laser-marking and documented engineering verification.
How do you guarantee clearance for Big Brake Kits (Brembo / AP Racing)? Before production or dispatch, the engineering team cross-checks your vehicle's brake caliper template against the wheel's internal X-Factor barrel profile. Exact spoke-to-caliper clearance is confirmed in millimetres against the supplied vehicle and brake configuration—eliminating guesswork and avoidable spacer fixes. What happens if a customer damages a single wheel months later? Core tooling and replacement availability should be agreed at programme launch. Where the approved model remains in production or stock, partners and retail clients can request a single replacement wheel without automatically replacing the full set. Do you support blank-wheel CNC drilling and OEM/ODM private labeling? Blank-wheel machining can be specified for custom PCD (bolt pattern), offset (ET), and centre bore (CB), subject to the approved casting design and engineering limits. OEM/ODM options can include custom centre caps, rim-lip laser engraving, and branded export cartons. What warranty and quality traceability comes with each wheel? Ask for the production batch, load rating, inspection ID and applicable test documentation before approval.
