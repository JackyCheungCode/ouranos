01 / ENGINEERING RESPONSE
Engineering the details that matter. A controlled manufacturing route is designed to address three critical areas: internal integrity, load distribution and surface durability.
STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY Control porosity at the source. Closed-loop low-pressure casting introduces molten A356.2 aluminium under regulated pressure. The process is designed to support a dense, consistent casting structure. Model-specific inspection plans can include X-ray evaluation and helium leak testing before CNC machining. A356.2 Aluminium · T6 Heat Treatment · Defined NDT Inspection Plan*
WEIGHT OPTIMISATION Material placed with purpose. Finite Element Analysis (FEA) can be used to evaluate cornering, radial and impact load cases. The objective is to identify low-stress areas for considered material reduction while maintaining strength around critical spoke-to-rim transitions. FEA Load Simulation · Brake Clearance Validation · Model-Specific Review
SURFACE DURABILITY A finish built in layers. A typical finish route may combine chemical pretreatment, primer, colour application and a protective clearcoat. The exact coating stack and validation programme are defined by the selected finish system and intended market. Pretreatment · Primer · Colour Layer · Protective Clearcoat*

How is clearance for Big Brake Kits verified? The vehicle and brake configuration should be checked against the wheel's internal barrel and spoke profile.
