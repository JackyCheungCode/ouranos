03 / ENGINEERING PHILOSOPHY ENGINEERING IS WHERE
EVERYTHING BEGINS.

01

Structural integrity

Design decisions begin with the intended application, load requirements and the relevant engineering constraints.

02

Weight optimisation

03

Vehicle compatibility

Fitment is assessed through the complete specification — not bolt pattern alone.

04

Design integrity

Distinctive form is developed within the boundaries of a defined construction and application.

05 / MATERIALS & ENGINEERING EVERY SPECIFICATION
HAS A PURPOSE. Engineering detail is not decoration. It defines how a wheel is designed, manufactured, inspected and matched to its intended vehicle.
01

Material science

Alloy grade, material condition and mechanical requirements defined by the approved product specification.

02

Structural design

Spoke geometry, rim sections, mounting interfaces and load requirements considered as one system.

03

Precision machining

Critical mounting and fitment dimensions controlled against the released drawing.

04

Vehicle fitment

Diameter, width, PCD, centre bore, offset, fasteners and brake clearance reviewed together.

05

Surface engineering

Preparation, coating specification, colour consistency and finish inspection considered throughout the programme.

06 / CUSTOMER CHALLENGES WE UNDERSTAND
WHAT CAN GO WRONG.
Trust starts by recognising the risks that matter — and defining the checks, information and decisions needed to manage them.
01 / INTERNAL INTEGRITY

WHAT YOU CANNOT SEE MATTERS MOST.

Surface appearance alone cannot confirm internal quality. Material and process controls, together with appropriate inspection and validation, matter.

02 / UNSPRUNG MASS

LESS WEIGHT. WITH ENGINEERING CONTEXT.

Weight optimisation must be considered alongside load, stiffness, fatigue requirements and the intended application.

03 / FINISH DURABILITY

A FINISH MUST OUTLAST THE FIRST IMPRESSION.

Surface preparation, coating specifications, process consistency and suitable durability checks all contribute to long-term appearance.

04 / BRAKE CLEARANCE

FITMENT IS MORE THAN BOLT PATTERN.

Spoke profile, offset, barrel geometry and brake package clearance must be reviewed for the specific vehicle.

05 / REPLACEMENT

ONE WHEEL SHOULD NOT BECOME A FOUR-WHEEL PROBLEM.

Model, specification and finish records help assess replacement options and reduce avoidable mismatch.

06 / OEM / ODM EXECUTION

YOUR SPECIFICATION. A CLEARER PATH TO PRODUCTION.

Defined requirements, drawing approval, sample review and production release help align expectations across a project.

07 / QUALITY ASSURANCE QUALITY MUST BE MEASURED.
NOT MERELY PROMISED.
The inspection plan should follow the product, manufacturing route, target market and agreed specification. Not every test applies to every wheel.
01

Material review

Specification and available batch documentation.

02

Process control

Defined manufacturing and in-process checkpoints.

03

Dimensional inspection

Critical geometry measured against approved drawings.

04

Product validation

Applicable tests and fitment checks for the programme.

05

Final review

Finish, identification and order requirements checked.

Evidence matters.

Test reports, certification status, inspection records and product scope should be reviewed for the specific model and order. OURANOS does not imply that every product holds the same certification or has completed the same validation programme.
