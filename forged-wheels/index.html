09 / KNOW THE DETAILS Frequently asked questions.
What is a forged wheel? Forged wheels begin with a metal billet or preform shaped under high pressure, then machined to the required geometry. Exact material and process details should be confirmed for each product series. What is the difference between Monoblock and multi-piece? Monoblock wheels are formed as one piece. Two-piece and three-piece designs use separate components assembled into a complete wheel. The appropriate construction depends on design and vehicle requirements. Can I customize the finish and details? Available finishes and personalization options vary by model. The OURANOS team can confirm the options supported by your selected design. How do I confirm vehicle fitment? Use Find Your Fit to identify the vehicle and review the available wheel specifications. Final clearance and installation suitability should be confirmed for the exact vehicle configuration. Where can I find minimum order and lead-time information? These details are managed at product-series level and confirmed with the selected specification during the enquiry process.
