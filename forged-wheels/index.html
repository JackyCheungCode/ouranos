02 / FORGED ARCHITECTURE Three constructions.
Distinct design possibilities.
The construction is not a label. It influences the wheel's visual depth, component layout and the way its details are resolved.
01 / ONE-PIECE

Monoblock

A unified form machined from one forged blank. A direct, continuous expression of spoke and barrel.

02 / TWO-PIECE

Two-Piece

A composed centre and barrel, allowing the face and outer profile to be considered as distinct elements.

03 / THREE-PIECE

Three-Piece

A multi-component construction in which the face, inner barrel and outer barrel form one assembled design.

Images are visual references for construction styles, not representations of confirmed OURANOS product models. Confirm the construction available for each selected model.

07 / DETAILS THAT MATTER Frequently asked
questions.
Which forged construction should I choose?Monoblock, Two-Piece and Three-Piece constructions offer different design and component architectures. The appropriate choice depends on the selected model, vehicle application and desired appearance. OURANOS can confirm the available construction for each design. Can the wheel be specified for my vehicle?Vehicle-specific dimensions should be reviewed together with the vehicle model, brake package and intended setup. Use Find Your Fit to begin the process; final fitment requires confirmation against the exact configuration. What can I personalize?Finish, machining details, engraving and center cap options may be available depending on the model and construction. The OURANOS team will confirm the applicable choices.
